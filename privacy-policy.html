At Tatva Nirman Infra Pvt. Ltd., we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal or professional information you share when interacting with our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you choose to provide directly when filling out our enquiry forms, requesting project credentials, or applying for vendor/career opportunities. This includes your name, company/organisation name, email address, phone number, and project details.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used strictly to:

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal data. Information is shared only with internal authorized teams or trusted operational partners strictly as needed to respond to your official business requirements.

4. Data Security

We maintain reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.
