How we handle information when you visit our website or contact us about infrastructure services. Please ensure this page accurately reflects your actual business practices before publishing.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, company or organisation name, business email address, contact number, location, and details about your project or enquiry.

Depending on the website's hosting and configuration, technical information may also be collected, such as IP address, browser and device details, pages visited, referring source, and access times. We collect such information only through technologies and services actually used on our website.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, understand water treatment, sewerage, infrastructure, or Solar EPC requirements, communicate about services and potential business opportunities, operate and improve the website, maintain security, keep business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, performance measurement, or analytics if these tools are enabled. You may manage cookies through your browser settings. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

Before publishing: Keep this section only if it accurately reflects the technologies used on your live website.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with authorised personnel and service providers supporting website hosting, maintenance, security, communications, or business operations. We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights or address unlawful activity. We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, anonymise it, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable law and our retention practices.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information relating to you, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or exercise other rights available under applicable data protection law. Contact us using the details below to submit a request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

7. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We do not control their privacy practices, security, or content. Please review the relevant policies before sharing information with a third party.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended primarily for business, professional, institutional, and project-related audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances where collection was not appropriate, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

9. Data Location and Transfers

Depending on the hosting infrastructure and third-party services used, information may be processed or stored outside your state or country. Where applicable law imposes requirements on such transfers, we will take steps required by that law.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our business, website features, technology, or legal requirements. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
